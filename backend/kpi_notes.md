# E-Commerce KPI Dashboard Metrics

## 1. Total Revenue
Total sales from completed orders.

Business meaning:
Shows how much money the store generated.

## 2. Average Order Value
Total revenue divided by number of completed orders.

Business meaning:
Shows how much customers spend per order on average.

## 3. Customer Acquisition Cost Estimate
Assume the business spends $50 to acquire each customer.

Business meaning:
Helps estimate whether customer value is higher than acquisition cost.

## 4. Customer Lifetime Value
Average total revenue generated per customer.

Business meaning:
Shows how valuable the average customer is.

## 5. Repeat Purchase Rate
Percentage of customers who placed 2 or more orders.

Business meaning:
Shows whether customers come back after their first purchase.

## 6. Product Category Performance
Revenue grouped by product category.

Business meaning:
Shows which product categories drive the most sales.

## 7. Revenue Trend
Daily revenue over the last 90 days.

Business meaning:
Shows whether sales are growing, declining, or unstable.

## 8. Top 5 Products by Revenue
Products generating the most revenue.

Business meaning:
Helps the owner know what products to promote, restock, or feature.
